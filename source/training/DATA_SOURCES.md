## Notes for any creator's footage

- **Ask for the original files** rather than downloading from YouTube. YouTube's terms forbid
  third-party downloaders even when the creator agrees, and the originals are better anyway:
  higher bitrate and often 60 fps, without YouTube's compression.
- **Confirm the scope in writing**: training the AI, publishing results, and whether clips may go
  into a released dataset (Vertex Bench) or only be used internally.
- **Credit** the channel wherever the footage is used (logs, paper, dataset card).
- **Players in the videos** are visible by username. Use the footage for training and evaluation
  only; anonymise names in anything published.
- Tier labels (LT2, HT2, ...) and round scores in titles are useful labels: skill band and outcome.
